# Peri-Workout Fueling: Pre, Intra, and Post-Workout Nutrition

## Pre-Workout Nutrition Strategy
The primary goal of pre-workout nutrition is maximizing liver and muscle glycogen stores and ensuring sustained blood glucose availability during exercise.
- 2 to 3 Hours Prior: Consume a balanced meal containing complex low-glycemic carbohydrates (oats, brown rice, sweet potatoes), lean protein (chicken, eggs, tofu), and minimal dietary fat/fiber to facilitate comfortable gastric emptying.
- 30 to 45 Minutes Prior: For high-intensity sessions lasting >60 minutes, ingest 20 to 35 grams of rapid-digesting, high-glycemic carbohydrates (banana, dried fruit, rice cakes) paired with 5-10g essential amino acids or whey isolate to prevent intra-workout catabolism.

## Intra-Workout Supplementation
For workouts exceeding 60 to 75 minutes in duration, or prolonged endurance sessions in high heat:
Consume 30 to 60 grams of easily digestible carbohydrates per hour (maltodextrin, cyclic dextrin, or electrolyte-carbohydrate sports drink) alongside 300 to 500mg sodium to sustain blood glucose oxidation rates and delay central neural fatigue.

## Post-Workout Recovery and the Anabolic Window
Immediately following strenuous exercise, muscle cells exhibit heightened insulin sensitivity and upregulated GLUT-4 transporter translocation. 
- Target Intake: Consume 25 to 40 grams of fast-digesting high-biological-value protein (whey protein isolate, egg whites, or soy isolate) combined with 0.5 to 1.0 g/kg carbohydrates within 45 to 60 minutes post-workout.
- Mechanism: Carbohydrate ingestion elevates insulin, which works synergistically with amino acids to suppress exercise-induced muscle protein breakdown (MPB) and supercompensate depleted glycogen reserves.
